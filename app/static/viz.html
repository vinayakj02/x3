x3 · data visualizations

Meaningful analytics on your solve data — hover to inspect.

01Trend & rolling averages

Every solve in order, with the overall mean and your rolling ao5 / ao12 overlaid. The core "am I improving?" view.

02Distribution histogram

Where your times cluster. Tight bars = consistent; a fat tail = blowups. Mean, best, worst marked. Hover a bin.

03Rolling averages curve

How ao5 and ao12 evolved solve-by-solve, with your best rolling average marked. The smoothest improvement signal.

04Session PBs

Your running best so far — each green dot is a new personal best in this session. A pure learning curve.

05Deviation scatter

Every solve as distance from the mean, with a ±1σ band. Outliers and DNFs stand out instantly.

06Consistency box plot

Median, quartiles, whiskers and outliers. The five-number summary of your session at a glance.

07Penalty breakdown

How many clean solves, +2s and DNFs. DNF rate is your reliability score.

08Pace meter

Drag the target time — see what % of your solves beat it. Sub-X pacing at a glance.

09Sub-X breakdown

What fraction of solves are under each classic threshold (sub-8 to sub-20).

10Last 12 window

Zoom into the solve set that forms your current ao12, with the window average drawn as a line.

11Heat timeline

The whole session as a color strip — green fast to red slow. Spot hot/cold runs. Hover any cell.

12Time-of-day

Your solves by hour of day vs time. Reveals when you're sharpest. DNFs shown as red X.

13Delta vs mean

Fast (green, up) or slow (red, down) relative to your mean — consistency over pure speed.

14Consistency streaks

Green runs are solves better than your mean; the longest streak is bracketed. How long can you stay in flow?

15Session compare

Best and mean per session across all your sessions — which sessions were truly faster?
